PROGRAMMABILITY

Pioneering User Programmable Banking

Programmable banking represents the next stage in financial evolution. As money and value become an operating system, P A V E B A N K enables the power of code to deliver automation beyond what's possible with APIs and build on top of your money and assets.

Traditional Banking

Limited API access, manual processes, rigid systems that don't adapt to your business needs.

Programmable Banking

Upload code, automate workflows, and build powerful integrations directly into your banking infrastructure.

What is programmable banking?

Pave's programmable banking, the first true operating system for money, lets you upload code to automate and manage both digital and traditional assets and currency, enabling automation and integration beyond what's been possible.

Get Started
// Automate your banking
pave.createRule({
  trigger: "deposit_received",
  condition: "amount > 10000",
  actions: [
    "notify_treasury",
    "auto_allocate"
  ]
});
